What you will train

Five disciplines, woven into one hour that flows. You never pay separately for any of them.

Undo the desk

Posture correction

Hours at a desk round the upper back, pull the shoulders forward and tighten the neck. We trace each strain to its root cause, then practise precise, gentle movements that release tension and let your natural alignment return.

It is the quiet foundation of everything else. Better alignment means every squat, stretch and stride lands where it should.

Fun factAn adult head weighs roughly 4.5 to 5 kg. Tilted forward 30 degrees, a biomechanical study estimated the load on your neck climbs to around 18 kg.Hansraj, Surgical Technology International, 2014
Myth-buster"Sit up ramrod straight all day." The best posture is your next one. Variety and movement protect you far more than any single rigid position.

Inside and out

Yoga

Physically, it builds mobility, flexibility and a steady core. Mentally, it softens stress and leaves you grounded, energised and quietly positive.

Poses are taught for strength as much as softness: holding, balancing and breathing through effort. It is the thread that ties the week together, with mobility for the body and quiet for the mind.

Science noteReviews of controlled trials link regular yoga with lower perceived stress and improved balance and flexibility.
Myth-buster"I am not flexible enough for yoga." Flexibility is what yoga builds, not what it asks for at the door.

Heart and bones

Cardio and HIIT

Dynamic sequences lift your heart rate, build stamina and support fat loss. Jumping movements also load your bones, which matters more with every year past thirty. Every movement has a low-impact variation, so no one is left behind.

Short bursts and recoveries train the heart to rebound quickly, which is why you feel lighter on stairs, on the school run and at the end of a long day.

Science noteBone responds to impact. Trials in premenopausal women found short jump routines improved hip bone density.
Myth-buster"Sweating more burns more fat." Sweat is your body cooling itself. It tells you about the room, not your results.

Lean, capable, balanced

Strength and balance

Full-body dumbbell routines paired with targeted bodyweight work. It builds lean tone, functional strength and healthier bones, and sharpens the coordination you use every day, all in minimal time.

Think carrying shopping without strain, getting up from the floor with ease, and holding your own balance for decades.

Fun factWithout resistance training, adults lose roughly 3 to 8 percent of muscle mass per decade after thirty, and strength training is the proven way to slow it.
Myth-buster"Lifting will make me bulky." Women have far lower testosterone, so building large muscle takes years of specialised effort. Most women get lean, strong and defined.

From your own kitchen

Practical nutrition

Simple, home-cooked food made from everyday ingredients you already own. No exotic powders and no rigid rules, just guidance that fits your table and your family's meals.

We work with the food you love, adjusting portions, balance and timing so energy stays steady and cravings quiet down.

Fun factDal with rice or roti is a smart pairing. Grains and pulses each lack an amino acid the other supplies, so together they give your body a fuller protein profile.
Myth-buster"I need protein powder to build muscle." Dal, curd, paneer, eggs and sprouts can carry you a long way.

The journal

Notes on strength, movement and living well in a woman's body.

Why your shoulders ache by 4 pm

Posture

A desk invites the body to fold forward: the head drifts ahead of the spine, the upper back rounds and the chest closes. The neck and shoulders then work overtime to hold your head up, which is why the ache arrives like clockwork.

Start small. Every hour, draw your shoulder blades gently together and down, lengthen the back of your neck and take three slow breaths. In class we go further, strengthening the muscles that hold you tall and releasing the ones that pull you forward.

Strong after thirty: why jumping matters

Strength and bones

Bone responds to load. Gentle impact, such as skipping or small jumps, signals bones to stay dense, which becomes more valuable as the years pass. Muscle works alongside it, protecting joints and keeping daily life effortless.

If jumping is not right for you today, a low-impact variation delivers much of the same benefit. Progress is yours to pace, and every movement in class comes with an option.

Yoga is strength, not only softness

Yoga

Holding a pose asks for control, balance and breath, and builds strength in places a weights routine can miss. It is equally a daily reset for a busy mind.

Think of it as the thread that ties your week together: mobility for the body, quiet for the mind, and a reason to feel good before you have even finished.

What a home-cooked plate should look like

Nutrition

You do not need a special menu. Aim for a plate that holds a source of protein such as dal, curd, paneer, eggs or sprouts, plenty of vegetables, and a portion of roti or rice that suits your appetite and activity.

Eat slowly, drink water through the day and keep the foods you love. A way of eating you can keep will always beat a perfect plan you cannot.
